Privacy Policy

Ram Setu is published by Vanarsena. Effective date: 12 September 2026.

This Privacy Policy explains how the app accesses, collects, uses, and shares user and device data. The app does not access personal or sensitive user data.

What we collect

The app does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics.

Data stored only on your device

To run the game, the app stores on this device:

  • High score, total stones, and achievements
  • Sound, music, haptic, language, and tutorial settings
  • An interrupted run so a game can resume after the app is interrupted

This data never leaves the device except if you use your phone’s built-in device-to-device transfer. Vanarsena cannot read it.

Permissions

Vibrate is used only for optional haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

Sharing

We do not share user data with third parties. The app includes no advertising, analytics, or social SDKs.

Security

There is no server, so no data is sent over a network. Local settings remain on your device.

Retention and deletion

On-device data is kept until you delete it. You can erase it in Settings → Reset saved data, or by uninstalling the app. There are no accounts to delete.

Children

The app is a general-audience game. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.
