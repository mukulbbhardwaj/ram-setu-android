APP NAME (30)
Ram Setu

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80)
Tap stones to build Ram Setu, the sacred bridge from Rameswaram to Lanka.

FULL DESCRIPTION (4000)
Build the sacred floating bridge.

Ram Setu is a timing game inspired by the Ramayana. Stones drift toward the gold line. Tap them at the right moment so they join the Setu. Catch one on the line for a Perfect hit. Let one sink, and the run ends.

How to play
• Tap stones near the gold line
• Perfect hits land on the line
• Combos raise your score
• Speed rises as the bridge grows

Features
• Hindi and English
• Music, sound, and haptics you can turn off
• Achievements for combos, speed, and stones gathered
• The story of Nala, Nila, and the vanara army

Play offline. No ads. No accounts.

Jai Shri Ram.
