Ram Setu Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Ram Setu is an offline game published under the developer name shown on its Google Play listing. This policy describes how the game handles data.

Data we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game does not create accounts, show ads, use analytics, or send gameplay data to our servers.

Data stored on your device

High score, total stones, achievements, language, sound, music, and haptic settings are saved locally on your device so the game can remember your progress. This data stays on the device unless you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Android backup

If you have Android backup enabled, your device may include these local settings in a backup associated with your Google account. That is a system setting. We do not operate a backup server or receive that data.

Permissions

The game uses the vibration permission (VIBRATE) for optional haptic feedback. You can turn haptics off in Settings.

Children

Ram Setu does not collect personal data from anyone, including children. It is a casual single-player game with no social features, chat, or ads.

Contact

Use the support email shown on the Ram Setu Google Play listing.
